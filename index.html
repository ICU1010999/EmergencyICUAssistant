About the Project

This educational prototype helps nursing students practice structured emergency assessment using selected patient observations and symptoms.

Important: This is a student learning tool, not a validated clinical decision-support system. Do not use it to diagnose, triage, or treat real patients.

      Clinical References

      The following resources support educational learning about emergency assessment. They do not validate this prototype's simplified decision rules.

      • WHO & ICRC — Basic Emergency Care

        ABCDE assessment, breathing difficulties, shock, and altered mental status.

      • NICE — Acutely Ill Adults in Hospital

        Recognition of and response to clinical deterioration.

      • Glasgow Coma Scale — Official Educational Resource

        Structured assessment of level of consciousness.

      • WHO — Emergency Care Toolkit

        Emergency care resources and structured clinical checklists.

      Academic note: This prototype is educational only. Its thresholds are simplified, have not been clinically validated, and must not be used to make real patient triage or treatment decisions.
